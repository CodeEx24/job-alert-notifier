# WD-73 — The popup shows the account, when it last synced, and what is waiting

**Epic:** EP-09 · **Wave:** 3 · **Priority:** P1

## Overview

As a user, I want the popup to show my connected account, last-synced time,
and any queued-but-not-yet-synced count, so I can tell at a glance whether my
data is actually reaching WatchDesk.

All of this was already known to the extension, and shown in two places: the
account card at the top (WD-42) said "Connected to WatchDesk" with the email
on its second line, and a separate line above the watch list (WD-54, WD-59,
WD-60) carried two "synced" times, the waiting count and the warnings.

They are now one status area, the account card:

```
● ada@example.com
  Chrome on Windows
  Last synced 2m ago · Nothing waiting to be sent
```

- The card's title is the account's email, or "Not connected to WatchDesk"
  with the Connect Account button, or "Connecting…" while a token is stored
  and WatchDesk has not yet said whose it is.
- The sync line moved into the card. It starts with one "Last synced Xm ago"
  label and then says how many listings are waiting and anything in the way.
- With no account connected the sync line is hidden and the popup is as it
  was.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Popup header shows the connected account's email (or 'not connected').
      — `describeConnection()` in `popup-account.js`: the title is the email
      when connected and "Not connected to WatchDesk" otherwise. Tests
      (`popup-account`): "connected shows the account's email as the title,
      and the device name beneath", "shows the email, or 'not connected' with
      the Connect Account button, in the card at the top", "connected before
      WatchDesk has named the account says 'Connecting…'", "a revoked token
      (401) reads not connected, says why, and offers Connect Account".
      "Header" is read as the card directly under the title bar (decision 1).
- [x] A 'last synced Xm ago' label and a pending-queue count (from US-025) are
      visible.
      — `describeWatchSync()` / `renderWatchSync()` in `popup-watch-sync.js`.
      Tests (`popup-watch-sync`): "connected and current … reads 'Last synced
      …' and that nothing is waiting", "'last synced' is the later of the
      watch sync and the listing upload" (five cases), "shows 'last synced'
      and the pending count for a connected account", "says how many listings
      are waiting after a send that failed", "as the minutes pass, moves the
      time and does not rewrite the state". The count is
      `getListingSyncStatus().queued`, the number of listings in this
      account's queue (WD-60), unchanged.

## Decisions taken

The handoff said to proceed without stopping, so I took these myself. Each is
the option I would have recommended and is open to challenge at review.

1. **The "header" is the account card**, the first thing under the title
   bar, not the `<header>` element (icon, name, settings button). The card
   already held the account and the connect action; putting the email in the
   title bar would have meant restructuring markup two other tickets are
   editing.
2. **The sync line moved into the card rather than a second indicator being
   added.** The element keeps its id (`watch-sync-status`) and `popup.js` is
   not touched: it still calls `renderWatchSync()` from the same places, and
   its existing 30-second tick keeps the label current. Only the
   refused-change message (`watch-change-error`) stays above the watch list,
   next to the control that caused it.
3. **"Last synced" is the later of two times**: the last sync that got the
   account's watch list (`watchdeskWatchSync.lastSyncedAt`) and the last
   check cycle after which no listing was left to send
   (`watchdeskListingSync.lastIngestedAt`). "Never synced" before either.
   - The popup syncs the watch list every time it opens, so with WatchDesk
     reachable the label usually reads "just now". When listings are failing
     while the watch list still syncs, the label is recent and the text
     beside it says listings couldn't be sent and how many are waiting; it no
     longer says when listings last got through (WD-59's "(last synced 1h
     ago)"). If the label should mean listings only, it is a one-line change
     in `lastSyncedAt()`.
4. **The count is always said**: "N listings waiting to be sent", or "Nothing
   waiting to be sent" for zero, rather than hiding zero. The criterion asks
   for a visible count, and words for zero mean the ok state is not carried
   by the dot's colour alone. Exception: offline with nothing queued, where
   the offline sentence is already long.
5. **Relative times left the state text.** The text is the live region
   (`role="status"`); the "Last synced" label is a sibling outside any live
   region (and not `aria-hidden`, so it can still be read). The card's
   `aria-live` moved from the whole section to its title and detail, since a
   live region around the label would announce every tick. Offline therefore
   reads "Your watches are shown as last synced." with the time in the label.
6. **A 403 says what to do.** `listing-ingest.js` now records
   `reason: "forbidden"` beside `failed` when a cycle ended on a 403, and the
   line reads "WatchDesk isn't accepting listings from this account. Verify
   your email address on WatchDesk · N listings waiting to be sent". The
   field is written only for a failed cycle and only that value is read
   back, so the stored shape is unchanged in every other case. Nothing about
   how a 403 is handled changed (WD-110).
7. **"Connecting…"** is shown for a connected state with no email and no
   display name. The card then asks the worker for the account once
   (`account-refresh`); the answer is the email or "Couldn't load the
   account's email right now.". Before, a pairing approved with the popup
   open stayed on "Connected" with no name until it was reopened.
8. **The device label keeps its own line** under the email. It was beside
   the email before.
9. **The dropped-listings wording is unchanged**, so it agrees with WD-60
   and its acknowledgement flow.

## Implementation details

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `popup.html` | The sync line is inside `#account-card`, as `#watch-sync-last` (the label) and `#watch-sync-text` (`role="status"`). `aria-live` is on `#account-title` and `#account-detail` instead of the section. |
| `popup-account.js` | `describeConnection()`: email as the title, "Connecting…", the could-not-load case. `renderAccountCard()` rewrites the title only when it changes and hides an empty detail line. `initAccountCard()` asks once for an unnamed account. |
| `popup-watch-sync.js` | `describeWatchSync()` returns `{ tone, lastSynced, text }`; `renderWatchSync(status, doc, now)` takes the clock. |
| `listing-ingest.js` | `reason: "forbidden"` in `watchdeskListingSync` and in `getListingSyncStatus()`. |
| `popup.css` | The sync line as the card's last line; hidden unless the card says connected, so a revoked token never leaves it beside "Not connected". |

No change to `popup.js`, `background.js`, `sites.js`, the manifest, or any
message. The popup still holds no state of its own: every render is from the
worker's answer, which it reads from `chrome.storage`. The token is not in
any of it.

## API changes

None.

## Known limitations

- See decision 3: with listings failing and the watch list syncing, "Last
  synced" is recent.
- The 403 message assumes an unverified email, which is the only cause today
  (`watchdesk-api.js`). Another cause of a 403 would get the same advice.
- A watch upload refused with a 403 during a sync still shows only as "N
  watches are only in this browser".
- The dropped warning's wording for listings WatchDesk refused (WD-110's
  follow-up) is still open.

## Testing notes

`npm run lint` is clean (`check-syntax` parses 15 of 15 files) and `npm test`
passes **602 tests in 10 files** (580 before).

States covered: unconnected; connecting; connected and current; a long queue
going out; offline with a queue; 403; token revoked (401); dropped listings;
and that the state text is not rewritten as the clock moves (a
MutationObserver over 20 ticks).

**Seen in a browser:** the real `popup.html`, `popup.css` and `popup.js`
served from a local static server and opened in headless Edge, with a stub
`chrome` object answering the popup's messages in place of the service
worker. Ten states were rendered and looked at (dark theme only).

**Not verified:** the extension was not loaded unpacked, so the real service
worker did not supply the state, and nothing was run against a WatchDesk
server (the handoff rules production out). No screen reader was used; the
live-region behaviour is asserted on the markup and on DOM writes. The light
theme was not looked at.

## Manual verification (Chrome)

1. Load the repository root unpacked, pointed at a local WatchDesk
   (`WATCHDESK_ENV = "development"`; do not commit that).
2. Open the popup: "Not connected to WatchDesk" and **Connect Account**.
3. Connect. The card shows "Connecting…" briefly, then the account's email,
   with "Last synced just now · Nothing waiting to be sent" beneath.
4. Stop the WatchDesk server and press **Check now**: the line turns amber,
   says "Offline" or that listings couldn't be sent, and counts what is
   waiting. Start the server and check again: it returns to "Nothing waiting
   to be sent".
5. Revoke the device on WatchDesk and reopen the popup: "Not connected to
   WatchDesk", the reason, no sync line.
